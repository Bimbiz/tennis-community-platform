# Supervisor's Review

The graduation project by Timur Bizinskiy and Arlan Tulegenov, “Development of a Mobile Application for the Amateur Tennis Community with Automatic Rating Calculation and Matching of Pairs,” addresses the organization of amateur tennis tournaments. Players currently rely on messaging groups and informal communication to find tournaments, partners and opponents. The topic is practical, and the planned rating-based matchmaking and automatic formation of doubles pairs give the project a reasonable level of engineering complexity.
The project scope is clearly outlined, and responsibilities are divided between mobile and backend development. The repository structure follows the required organization. The development workflow is defined: YouTrack tasks, issue-named branches, pull requests and peer review.
At the same time, the submission reflects an early stage of work. The problem statement is general and is not supported by data or evidence from potential users. The following required components are not reflected in the repository: expected outcomes, evidence of relevance, the verification and evaluation plan, and supervisor feedback. The supervisor is not specified. Key technology decisions, including the database, local storage and the rating algorithm, are still under evaluation. The current status describes planned activities rather than completed deliverables.
Overall, the work partially meets the requirements of the first pre-defense and can be graded “good.”
