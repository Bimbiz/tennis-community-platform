# User Interview Guide

**Project:** Amateur Tennis Community Platform  
**Purpose:** To understand current experiences, difficulties, and needs related to amateur tennis matches, skill assessment, and tournament participation.  
**Interview type:** Semi-structured  
**Estimated duration:** 10-15 minutes

## Interview Questions

1. **Tennis participation:** How do you currently find people to play tennis with, or organize matches for others?
2. **Opponent matching:** What, if anything, makes it difficult to find suitable opponents or playing partners? Can you describe a recent example?
3. **Skill assessment:** How do you currently assess your own or another player's skill level? What information do you rely on?
4. **Ratings:** Have you used any player rating or level system? If so, how did it affect your choice of matches or tournaments? If not, how do you decide whether players are at similar levels?
5. **Tournament experience:** Have you participated in or organized amateur tennis tournaments? If yes, how did registration, scheduling, and communication work? If not, what has influenced your decision not to participate?
6. **Tournament difficulties:** Based on your experience, what parts of joining or running a tournament are most inconvenient, if any?
7. **Singles and doubles:** How do you usually find a doubles partner, if you play doubles? What would you consider important when choosing or being assigned a partner?
8. **Mobile app needs:** If you used a mobile app for amateur tennis activities, which tasks would you want it to help with, and why?
9. **Priorities and concerns:** Which of the needs we discussed matter most to you? What concerns, if any, would affect your willingness to use such an app?

## Interviewer Guidance

- Ask the questions conversationally; adapt the order when appropriate.
- Use neutral follow-ups such as "Could you tell me more?", "Can you give an example?", and "What happened next?"
- Do not explain or promote proposed app features before gathering the respondent's current experience.
- Record what the respondent actually says; distinguish observations from your interpretations.
- Participation is voluntary. Do not collect unnecessary personal information; ask permission before recording audio or identifying someone in a report.
